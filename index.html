The problem

Everyone on your team is using AI.
Nobody's using the same playbook.

🌀

Skills live in silos

Your best AI prompts and workflows are stuck in individual clipboards, personal Notion docs, and private ChatGPT histories. When that person leaves — the knowledge walks out with them.

🔁

No version control

Prompts get tweaked and improved — but there's no record of what changed, why it changed, or which version produced the result you're trying to replicate.

📊

Outputs disconnected from results

You can see performance data. You can see the creative. But tracing a result back to the specific skill version that produced it? That requires manual sleuthing no one has time for.

⏱️

Teams reinvent the wheel constantly

Without a shared library of proven skills, every team member starts from scratch. You're paying for the same discovery — in time and ad spend — over and over again.

Features

Everything your team needs to scale AI output

One platform to capture, version, share, and measure every AI skill your team creates.

🗄️

Skills Vault

A centralized, searchable library of every prompt, workflow, and AI skill your team has ever built. Never lose a winning formula again.

🔀

Version Control

Every change tracked, every iteration saved. See exactly what changed between versions and roll back to any point in a skill's history.

📈

Performance Linking

Connect your AI outputs to real results. See which skill version produced which creative — and tie it directly to ROAS, CTR, and conversion data.

👥

Team Collaboration

Share skills across your team with role-based permissions. Editors iterate, admins approve, everyone builds on what actually works.

🏷️

Auto-Naming Engine

Outputs are named and tagged automatically based on the skill that created them, keeping your asset library clean and traceable at scale.

🔌

Ad Platform Sync

Direct integrations with Meta Ads and more. Performance data flows in automatically — no manual exports, no spreadsheet archaeology.

How it works
